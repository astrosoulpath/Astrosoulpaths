# Privacy Policy for Astro Soul Path

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Astro Soul Path respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Account and authentication data
We collect the mobile phone number you submit for sign-in and one-time password verification. After successful verification, we may store authentication session data required to keep you signed in securely.

### 2. Profile and astrology data
If you complete onboarding or related astrology features, we may collect information you provide such as:
- full name
- date of birth
- time of birth
- place of birth
- gender
- related profile completion status

This data is used to create your account profile and support kundli, astrology, and personalization features.

### 3. Location data
If you choose the current-location option, the app requests access to your device location in order to resolve your city or birth location details. Location access is optional and is only used when you actively request it.

### 4. Microphone data
If you use in-app calling features, the app requests microphone access so audio calls can function. Microphone access is used only while a call feature is active.

### 5. Notification data
The app may request permission to send notifications. Notifications may be used for account events, onboarding feedback, reminders, and future product updates.

### 6. Device and diagnostic data
We may process limited technical information such as app version, device type, operating system, and error logs to maintain app stability, security, and performance.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
- verify your account and sign you in
- maintain a secure authenticated session
- create and manage your profile
- provide astrology, kundli, and onboarding features
- resolve location-based birth details when requested
- support audio calling features
- send relevant notifications if you grant permission
- debug, secure, and improve the app

## Legal Basis and Consent

Where required by applicable law, we rely on your consent for permissions such as location, microphone, and notifications. You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

## How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in the following cases:
- with service providers and infrastructure partners that support authentication, backend APIs, data storage, notifications, or analytics
- when required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request
- to protect the security, rights, or integrity of the app, our users, or the public
- as part of a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

## Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain account security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

## Your Choices

You can:
- choose not to grant location, microphone, or notification permissions
- stop using optional features that depend on those permissions
- request deletion or correction of your data, subject to applicable law and operational requirements
- sign out of the app

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

Astro Soul Path is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law in your region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, depending on the location of our service providers or infrastructure. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be applied.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may update the date above and provide additional notice where appropriate.
